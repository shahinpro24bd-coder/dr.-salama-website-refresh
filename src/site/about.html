Who is Dr. Husien Salama?

Dentist in Baghdad | Dr. Husien Salama — Saving Teeth Is My Mission

Endodontics & Restorative Dentistry

Dr. Husien Salama is a dentist at Clear Dental Care in Al-Mansour, Baghdad, followed by more than 63,000 people on Instagram for one clear message: save the natural tooth whenever it can be saved.

He holds a Diploma in Dental Treatment from the ADA and treats every case with x-rays, rubber dam isolation and magnification — because the inside of a tooth matters as much as how it looks.

His work includes root canal treatment and retreatment, removal of broken instruments, deep margin elevation, fiber posts and lab-made IPS e.max restorations.

Patients travel from across Iraq — including Anbar — to save teeth they were told to extract. Many complex cases are completed in just two or three visits.

“The right treatment at the right time changes a tooth's fate completely.” — Dr. Husien Salama

Our Treatments

Endodontic & Restorative Treatments

Root Canal Treatment

Careful cleaning and sealing of infected canals to relieve pain and keep your natural tooth.

Root Canal Retreatment

Redoing failed or under-filled root canals by finding and treating the real cause of failure.

IPS e.max Lab Restorations

Lab-made ceramic inlays and onlays that restore strength and a natural look.

Deep Margin Elevation

Raising deep cavity margins with modified bands for a precise, sealed restoration.

Broken Instrument Removal

Bypassing or retrieving separated files inside canals to finish treatment safely.

Fiber Post & Core

Reinforcing root-treated teeth with fiber posts before the final restoration.

Aesthetic Fillings

Tooth-coloured composite fillings that blend invisibly with your smile.

Rubber Dam Isolation

Every procedure under rubber dam for a clean, sterile and bacteria-free field.

Digital X-ray Diagnosis

Precise radiographs to detect hidden infection and bone loss early.

Your Patient Journey

How We Save Your Tooth

Your Visit, Step by Step

1. Examination & X-ray
First visit

We listen to your complaint and take a digital x-ray to see infection, bone loss or old failed treatment.

2. Clear Treatment Plan
Honest options

You see your x-ray, understand the problem and choose a plan — saving the tooth always comes first.

3. Treatment Under Isolation
Precise work

Root canal or restoration under rubber dam and magnification, often finished in just two or three visits.

4. Final Restoration
Lasting result

The tooth is rebuilt with a fiber post, composite or a lab-made e.max restoration to protect it for years.

5. Follow-up
Long-term care

Review visits and control x-rays make sure the tooth stays healthy and pain-free.

About Dr. Husien Salama

Dr. Husien Salama is a dentist practising at Clear Dental Care in Al-Mansour, Baghdad. He holds a Diploma in Dental Treatment (ADA) and has built his practice around one goal — saving natural teeth that others would extract.

His daily work focuses on endodontics and conservative dentistry: root canal treatment and retreatment, removal of broken instruments, deep margin elevation, fiber posts and lab-made IPS e.max restorations. “The right treatment at the right time changes a tooth's fate completely.”
