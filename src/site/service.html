Endodontics & Restorative Dentistry

Saving Teeth Is My Mission. Our Treatments

Every treatment begins with an x-ray and an honest conversation. A precise diagnosis, a plan built around saving your tooth, and careful technique for results that last.
Root Canal Treatment
Careful cleaning and sealing of infected canals to relieve pain and keep your natural tooth.
When decay or a crack reaches the nerve, the tooth becomes painful and infected. Dr. Husien cleans, shapes and disinfects every canal under rubber dam isolation, then seals it precisely — guided by x-rays at every step — so the tooth stays in your mouth for years.
Read More
Root Canal Retreatment
Redoing failed or under-filled root canals by finding and treating the real cause of failure.
A root canal can fail years later because of missed canals, short fillings or leaking restorations. Before retreating, we find the reason for failure, remove the old gutta-percha, disinfect thoroughly and re-seal the canals — treating the cause, not just the result.
Read More
Saving Broken-Down Teeth
Rescuing badly decayed teeth from extraction with fiber posts and strong lab-made restorations.
Many teeth that are scheduled for extraction can still be saved. With root canal treatment, a fiber post and a lab-made restoration, Dr. Husien rebuilds badly broken teeth so they look and work like natural teeth again — “saving the tooth is my mission”.
Read More
IPS e.max Lab Restorations
Lab-made ceramic inlays and onlays that restore strength and a natural look.
IPS e.max lithium-disilicate restorations are made in the lab to fit your tooth exactly. They are strong, tooth-colored and conservative, protecting root-treated and heavily filled teeth while keeping as much natural structure as possible.
Read More
Deep Margin Elevation
Raising deep cavity margins with modified bands for a precise, sealed restoration.
When decay extends below the gum line, a precise restoration becomes difficult. Deep margin elevation — using techniques such as band modification — raises the margin to a cleanable level, giving a perfect seal for the final restoration.
Read More
Broken Instrument Removal
Bypassing or retrieving separated files inside canals to finish treatment safely.
A file broken inside a canal does not always mean losing the tooth. With patience, magnification and the right instruments, Dr. Husien bypasses or retrieves separated files and ledges, then completes cleaning and filling of the canal.
Read More
Rubber Dam Isolation
Every procedure under rubber dam for a clean, sterile and bacteria-free field.
Saliva carries bacteria that can re-infect a tooth during treatment. Working under rubber dam keeps the tooth dry and sterile, protects you from swallowing materials and makes bonding and root canal treatment far more predictable.
Read More
Fiber Post & Core
Reinforcing root-treated teeth with fiber posts before the final restoration.
A root-treated tooth that has lost much of its structure needs support. A fiber post, bonded inside the root, and a strong core build-up create a solid foundation for the final filling or e.max restoration.
Read More
Aesthetic Fillings
Tooth-colored composite fillings that blend invisibly with your smile.
Decay is removed carefully and the tooth is rebuilt layer by layer with tooth-colored composite, recreating its natural shape and grooves for a strong, invisible repair.
Read More
Digital X-ray Diagnosis
Precise radiographs to detect hidden infection and bone loss early.
A tooth can look healthy from outside while infection grows inside. Digital x-rays reveal inflammation, bone loss and failed old treatments early — sometimes before any pain — so the tooth can be saved in time.
Read More
Conservative Caries Care
Not every cavity needs drilling — arrested caries is monitored, not over-treated.
The right treatment is not always a filling. Arrested (inactive) caries can be left untouched and monitored. Dr. Husien explains what really needs treatment and what doesn't, so you keep the maximum of your healthy tooth.
Read More
Emergency Toothache Relief
Fast, calm relief for severe tooth pain and swelling, with treatment of the cause.
Severe toothache and swelling need quick, careful care. We diagnose the source with an x-ray, relieve the pain and start the right treatment — often the first step of a root canal that saves the tooth.
Read More
Check-up & Cleaning
Regular check-ups and professional cleaning to keep teeth and gums healthy.
Regular check-ups catch problems while they are small and easy to treat. Professional scaling and polishing remove tartar and stains, keep your gums healthy and protect the treatments you have already invested in.
Read More
Precise Diagnosis & Personal Planning
An honest assessment, precise diagnostics and a clear treatment plan.
Your journey begins with a consultation. We examine, take x-rays and explain every option clearly — saving your tooth always comes first, with no pressure and no surprises.

About Dr. Husien Salama

Dr. Husien Salama is a dentist practicing at Clear Dental Care in Al-Mansour, Baghdad. He holds a Diploma in Dental Treatment (ADA) and has built his practice around one goal — saving natural teeth that others would extract.

His daily work focuses on endodontics and conservative dentistry: root canal treatment and retreatment, removal of broken instruments, deep margin elevation, fiber posts and lab-made IPS e.max restorations. “The right treatment at the right time changes a tooth's fate completely.”
