About Dr. Husien Salama

Who is Dr. Husien Salama?

Dr. Husien Salama is a dentist practising at Clear Dental Care in Al-Mansour, Baghdad. He holds a Diploma in Dental Treatment (ADA) and has built his practice around one goal — saving natural teeth that others would extract.

His daily work focuses on endodontics and conservative dentistry: root canal treatment and retreatment, removal of broken instruments, deep margin elevation, fiber posts and lab-made IPS e.max restorations. “The right treatment at the right time changes a tooth's fate completely.”
